# Sutter reference microscopy workstation

The `sutter-mp285-reference` asset profile combines an independently modelled
Nikon TE2000-S stand with electric XYZ manipulators reconstructed from Sutter
MP-285 dimensions and XenoWorks installation illustrations. It needs no
downloaded CAD fixture. All scene geometry is generated by the same Python
builders for MuJoCo, Isaac export and optional Blender renders.

This is an engineering and appearance reference. It is not manufacturer CAD,
a complete one-to-one replica or a model of instrument positioning accuracy.
The [manufacturer source inventory](microscopy_oem_references.md) separates
published dimensions from missing interface information and records the
original-model search.

## Run

From the repository root, select the profile before starting the application:

```bash
HOOKE_MICROSCOPY_ASSETS=reference \
HOOKE_MICROSCOPY_MANIPULATOR=sutter-mp285-reference \
HOOKE_MICROSCOPY_STAND=te2000-s-reference \
HOOKE_MICROSCOPY_STAGE=reference \
HOOKE_MICROSCOPY_OPTICS=estimated \
PYTHONPATH=Hooke .venv/bin/python -m webui.server --host 0.0.0.0 --port 8084
```

Configure source/native GPU indices and the Isaac installation as described
in the [live-backend guide](microscopy_live_backends.md). `HOOKE_MICROSCOPY_STAND=auto`
also selects TE2000-S for this profile. The original default and explicit
parallel/CAD profiles remain available.

This mounting layout supports one tool or an opposed pair, covering all five
public cell tasks. Unsupported three-tool layouts and two tools assigned to
the same side fail explicitly instead of generating overlapping equipment.

## Geometry and operation

- Three articulated serial slides move the tool through the existing control
  interface. Each has 25 mm total travel. Fixed base, moving carriages,
  upright tower, locked angle dial, holder and exposed glass have separate
  assembly roles.
- The base's 63.5 mm square mounting pattern follows the MP-285 manual. The
  broad adjustable plate and lower dovetail follow XenoWorks illustrations.
  The stand-side bracket is an independent estimate because the BR-N27
  interface drawing remains unavailable.
- The holder uses the published 4 mm rod diameter and nominal 50 mm carrier
  length. Its 110 mm total holder length, detailed chuck shape and interfaces
  are estimates. Hoses attach to explicit moving ports rather than an assumed
  holder length.
- Suspended-cell injection has opposed horizontal terminal tips and 35°/45°
  holding/injection shafts. The estimated circular bend occupies arc lengths
  0.8–2 mm behind the mouth. Adherent-cell injection retains a straight 45°
  pipette. The 12 mm exposed glass length is independent of the holder size.
- In the forceps task, the unused injector is installed on the opposite side
  from the forceps. Its geometry and descriptive metadata use the same pose.
- The injection opening remains 1.2 µm outside / 0.5 µm inside. The suspended
  cell remains 36 µm across; the adherent cell remains 36 × 28 × 8 µm.
  Macroscopic presentation renders do not enlarge these objects.

The TE2000-S keeps the published stand envelope, eyepoint and 25° observation
angle. Revised binocular housings, eyetubes, controls, condenser and cast
surfaces follow the original outlines. An estimated inclined conical carousel
has six objective sockets; the selected 40X axis and sample/focus datums stay
unchanged. Its rear guide, low fork and spindle are independently designed
support geometry, not a Nikon or Q-545 drivetrain.

## Model boundaries

The profile name identifies an asset family independently of the control
topology. Pipette geometry explicitly records the holder direction, distal
direction, bend interval and glass base. Mesh generation, approach control,
cell force calculations and depth-dependent imaging consume this information.
The local bend is shown in 3D and projected into synthetic microscopy.

Slide masses, servo gains, mounting stiffness and detailed fasteners are not
manufacturer measurements. Small numerical masses on visual child bodies have
explicit gravity compensation. Isaac uses coordinates centred on the sample
to retain useful precision for micrometre-scale movements.

Cell contact is still the shared reduced membrane/holding model. The rigid
engines move the instruments; this revision does not introduce resolved cell
membranes, measured tissue response or fluid simulation. Manufacturer motor
resolution must not be interpreted as the simulation's validated accuracy.

## Local evidence

Generated scene snapshots, before/after images, editable Blender scenes,
native recordings and regression reports are kept under
`temp/microscopy_sutter/20260919/`. Raw manufacturer documents remain in
`temp/microscopy_oem_references/20260919/`. These directories are ignored by Git.

The 2026-09-19 validation used the same final geometry in both engines:

| Check | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Five public tasks in each engine | 10/10 episodes, 124/124 workflow checks |
| Archived contact, pressure, dose and motion consistency | 199/199 checks across 86,987 recorded steps |
| Python regression cases, including targeted fixes | 150 passed; 11 optional cases and one optional group skipped |
| New recordings | 20 MP4/WebM files fully decoded; 100 historical videos preserved |

The geometry clearance audit covers the five recorded MuJoCo trajectories
(43,493 steps). It found no unexpected instrument intersections; the declared
stand-bridge/casting engagement remains an estimated mounting interface. The
actual hollow pipettes cleared the sample glass and dish walls, with conservative
gap bounds of 6.06 µm for adherent-cell injection and 13.45 µm for suspended-cell
injection. These checks do not establish full-travel clearance or physical
mounting rigidity.

All injection episodes delivered the requested 0.5 pL in the reduced model.
During commanded delivery, the maximum simulated needle defocus in Isaac was
0.037 µm for adherent cells and 0.017 µm for suspended cells. These are numerical
observations of the model, not measured instrument accuracy. The recording
release is `20260919-sutter-reference`; observations are captured at 2 or 4 FPS
and held at 4 FPS without interpolating motion.
