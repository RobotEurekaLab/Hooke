## Newly confirmed facts beyond the existing card
- CMIC has opened a substantial new research building (referred to as "DCMIC" in the press text) with 88 laboratories dedicated to synthesis, materials processing, and analysis, plus a large student teaching lab with up to 40 fume hoods — confirming the department's "new research facilities at the Leonardo Campus" lead noted (but not fetched) in the original card.
- Visual ground-truth for at least 3 distinct room "types" now exists for this department: (1) a synthesis wet-lab with fume hood + wall-mounted glass cabinet storage, (2) an X-ray diffraction instrument room (PANalytical-brand), (3) a second XRD/single-crystal diffraction room (Rigaku-brand) with radiation shielding, and (4) a digital-fabrication/3D-printing lab. It is not confirmed which, if any, of these specific rooms is BiocatLab's own space (BiocatLab's focus is enzymatic/biocatalytic synthesis, closest to the wet-lab fume-hood photo), but all are real, current (recent press release) CMIC-department rooms in the same building complex as BiocatLab's stated address.
- A companion article (fuorisalone.it) indicates PoliMi also operates newer "B.Lab" (biofabrication/mycelium) and "Circular Fashion-Tech Lab" facilities at the separate Bovisa Durando campus — a different site, flagged to avoid conflating with the Via Mancinelli/Leonardo campus BiocatLab location.
