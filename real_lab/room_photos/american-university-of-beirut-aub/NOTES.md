## Newly confirmed facts beyond existing card
- The Molecular Diagnostics Laboratory was established at AUBMC in 2001 and
  has grown into the major referral center for genomic/molecular profiling
  in Lebanon, with technology spanning basic PCR through Sanger and
  Next-Generation Sequencing.
- The Genomic Profiling Program draws on 3 service units: Dept. of
  Pathology and Laboratory Medicine (incl. Molecular Diagnostics Lab and
  Medical Genetics Lab), the Basic Science Dept. at the Faculty of
  Medicine, and the Naef K. Basile Cancer Institute.
