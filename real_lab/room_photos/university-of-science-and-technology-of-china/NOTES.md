## What was found
**理化科学实验中心 (Physical & Chemical Sciences Experimental Center)**, founded 1979, is USTC's main shared instrumentation center: 4 platforms (electron microscopy/imaging, structure & physical properties, inorganic analysis, organic analysis), 88 instruments valued over 500k RMB each, total asset value ~268 million RMB, 46 staff. This is a strong candidate facility, but no interior/room photography was found -- only individual instrument product photos.
