## What was found
The "Labs and Equipment" page embeds four images under `/research/electron-microscopy/images/equipment/`, and all four turned out to be genuine interior photographs (not product shots) when downloaded and viewed:

1. **SamplePreparationLab.JPG** (saved as `linnaeusborg_sample_prep_lab.jpg`): Wide shot of an open-plan wet lab. Two adjustable chrome articulated fume-extraction arms hang from the ceiling over a central island bench. Bright yellow/orange vinyl flooring (distinctive — recurs in all four photos, clearly the building's signature flooring color). White laminate benches with black trim, a red wheeled stool, a chrome-footed lab stool, standard power-strip sockets along a raised backsplash shelf holding reagent bottles, a small centrifuge (Branson label visible), an eyewash/safety shower, waterproof trash bin, large window wall with blinds at the far end showing the building's own facade reflected, visible second workbench with a confocal/inverted microscope stand. Ceiling is suspended grid with fluorescent panel lighting; a support column with "no [chemical hazard]" signage.

2. **WetLab1.jpg** (saved as `linnaeusborg_wetlab1.jpg`): Panoramic shot of a long wet lab corridor-style room. Same yellow/orange flooring. A fume hood labeled "Secuflow" at left, an old beige desktop PC tower, shelving unit holding labelled reagent bottles over a bench with pipettors and a centrifuge, gas cylinder (black, tall) at right next to what appears to be a dishwasher/glassware washer unit, red step-stool, black adjustable lab chairs, a doorway leading to a sunlit corridor/further lab space in the background, window wall at right admitting daylight.

3. **WetLab2.jpg** (saved as `linnaeusborg_wetlab2.jpg`): Another wet lab room, same yellow flooring. Bench with white lab coat hanging on back of a chair, reagent shelving, a window wall with roller blinds partially down, red step-stool, second workstation with monitor/PC and what looks like a balance or small instrument, another chemical-safety-cabinet unit at right with amber/warning signage, waste bin.

4. **WetLab3.jpg** (saved as `linnaeusborg_wetlab3.jpg`): Close/mid shot — a tall glass-fronted refrigerated unit (labelled "UNEQUIP"/"UNICHROMAT 1000"?) at left, a "Secuflow" fume hood, a desk with PC/keyboard, red step-stool, bench at right cluttered with pipettors, bottles, a red binder, pH meter/analyzer, yellow pipette-tip racks — very "lived-in" working bench.

## Newly confirmed facts beyond existing card
- Exact building detail: Linnaeusborg **U-buildings 5171 & 5174, ground floor**, Nijenborgh 7, 9747 AG Groningen (prior card only said "Linnaeusborg" with no building/floor number).
- Distinctive, consistent visual signature across the whole EM/sample-prep suite: bright yellow/orange vinyl flooring, white laminate casework, articulated ceiling-mounted fume-extraction arms, red wheeled step-stools — a strong, reusable material/color palette for a 3D reconstruction even though these are "wet lab"/sample-prep rooms rather than the cryo-EM microscope room itself (the Talos Arctica/Tecnai 20 instrument rooms were not separately photographed on this page).
- Note: these photos show the *sample preparation* wet labs feeding the EM facility, not the microscope rooms housing the Talos Arctica/Tecnai 20 themselves — still extremely valuable room-level evidence for general "molecular biology wet lab" scenes, and strongly suggestive of the look of the broader GBB floor.
