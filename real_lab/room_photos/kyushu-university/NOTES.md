This is the clearest room-level shot: shows ceiling-height shelving, multiple surfaces, general room layout, dim/dark-room lighting typical of a laser lab.

NOT a lab interior -- kept for context only (confirms lab is active, headcount, campus setting) but not counted as the room-photo evidence.
