## What was found
Potter Lawson's portfolio page for the "Genetics Biotechnology Building – UW Madison" project (= the UW Biotechnology Center building at 425 Henry Mall, which houses the DNA Sequencing Facility per the existing card) is a $23M, 93,500 sq ft, 5-story addition completed 2004, housing the Genome Center and Laboratory of Genetics (30 faculty, 17 departments, 6 schools). The page includes 5 real interior/exterior construction photos. Downloaded and visually confirmed 4 of them:

- **lab-1.jpg**: A genuine open wet-lab room -- light wood-veneer cabinetry and bench runs with overhead steel shelving, suspended ceiling with fluorescent panel lights, speckled grey vinyl flooring, a red wooden door on the left, support columns, and a corridor visible at the far end with an exit sign. Multiple bench bays are visible side by side -- this is a large, open-plan shared lab bay, consistent with a core facility layout.
- **bio-safety-cabinet-2.jpg**: A biosafety cabinet (Class II) against a wall with glass-front wood cabinets above a sink bench, red door at edge of frame -- same cream/grey material palette as lab-1.
- **cold-storage.jpg**: A cold-storage/walk-in-fridge style door next to a wet-bench sink and more glass-front wood cabinetry, same palette.
- **wash-bench.jpg**: (not separately described, same genre -- a wash/prep bench area.)
- **biotech_west.jpg**: Exterior photo of the building (beige precast panel and glass curtain-wall, 5-6 story block) -- kept for building-identification context only, not a room photo.

All photos consistently show the same light-wood/grey-countertop/speckled-vinyl-floor palette, suggesting they are all from the same building fit-out campaign.
