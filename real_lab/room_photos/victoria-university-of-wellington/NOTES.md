## What was found -- CONFIRMED ROOM PHOTO
Downloaded `vucel-lab-microscope.jpg` (from `images/lab-microscope.jpg` on
the facilities page, 230x177 px -- a small/low-res image but genuinely
useful). It shows **two researchers in white lab coats working at a bench
lined with stereo/dissecting microscopes**, with sample containers and lab
equipment on the bench in front of them, and **a window behind them looking
out over the ocean** -- consistent with VUCEL's documented location
overlooking Cook Strait / the Taputeranga Marine Reserve in Island Bay.
This is a genuine room-level photo (bench, multiple microscopes, two
people, window/view context) rather than an equipment-only shot.

## Newly confirmed facts beyond existing card
- VUCEL is an 816 sqm facility including a PC-2 standard research
  laboratory, two wet labs with raw/filtered flow-through seawater access,
  and office space for up to 30 research students/staff -- located 8 km
  from the main Kelburn campus, in Island Bay, overlooking the Taputeranga
  Marine Reserve.
- The confirmed photo shows the lab bench setup with ocean-view windows, a
  distinctive visible feature not previously documented.
