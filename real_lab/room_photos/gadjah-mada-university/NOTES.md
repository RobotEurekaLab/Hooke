## What was found
- The UGM news article about the IGF launch contains 4 photos
  (`IGF-1-700x500.jpg`, `IGF-2-700x500.jpg`, `IGF-3-700x500.jpg`,
  `DSC00078-scaled-700x500.jpg`). Downloaded and inspected all four: **all
  four are photos of the launch ceremony** ("Rapat Terbuka Senat Fakultas
  Biologi UGM", Dies Natalis ke-69, 19 September 2024) held in a formal
  auditorium with banners, flowers, a podium, and people in academic gowns
  (sashes, mortarboards). None show the actual IGF lab room -- this is a
  senate/ceremonial event room, not the genome sequencing facility itself.
  Deleted after inspection as not relevant.
- The official igf-sequencing.com/facilities/ page lists only clean
  equipment product shots (ONT PromethION 24, PacBio Revio, Opentrons Flex,
  TapeStation, LightBench, Mupid exU, Qubit, spectrophotometer, HPC/GPU/
  storage racks) as isolated `.webp` product images -- no room context.

## Newly confirmed facts beyond existing card
- IGF was formally launched 19 September 2024 during UGM Faculty of Biology's
  69th Dies Natalis (anniversary), held in the Tropical Biology Auditorium.
  Confirmed location: 2nd Floor, Building B, Faculty of Biology UGM.
