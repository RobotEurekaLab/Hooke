## What was found

- **Educational_Conway-Inst_4_full-size-2.jpg**: a genuine interior room photo -- a long, open teaching/research lab with a continuous bench run of computer workstations (CRT/flat monitors visible) along a full-height glazed curtain wall looking out onto trees, grey countertops and cabinetry, suspended ceiling with linear fluorescent fixtures and sprinkler heads, and -- on the opposite wall -- a bank of wall-mounted wash-hand basins with soap/towel dispensers above them. Captioned on the source page as "science lab room with desks, sinks and computers."
- **Educational_Conway-Inst_2-2.jpg**: captioned as another lab interior (smaller/cropped thumbnail; not separately re-examined at full size but same source/caption family).

Kept for building-identification context, not counted as room evidence.
