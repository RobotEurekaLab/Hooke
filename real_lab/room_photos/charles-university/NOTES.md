## What was found
- The VMCF homepage's image tags were extracted directly via curl+grep: a
  banner graphic, a scientific specimen photomicrograph
  (`Eurytrema_hepatica_...`), the Czech-Bioimaging infrastructure logo, and
  two ambiguous-filename images. Downloaded and inspected the two ambiguous
  ones (`f6tv7p4odhxsc1um1rrf.jpg`, `irpnetj19uawn1jjzeus.jpg`): both turned
  out to be **scientific microscopy result images** (a black-and-white SEM
  image of pollen-like structures, and a colorized fluorescence/confocal-
  style image of what resembles a virus particle) -- i.e. example outputs
  produced using the facility's microscopes, not photos of the facility
  room itself. Both deleted after inspection.
- Viničná Microscopy Core Facility is located at Viničná 7, Prague 2,
  Faculty of Science, and includes a Laboratory of Electron Microscopy,
  Laboratory of Confocal and Fluorescence Microscopy, and Laboratory of
  Flow Cytometry (Room S007) -- but no interior photos of any of these
  rooms were found.
