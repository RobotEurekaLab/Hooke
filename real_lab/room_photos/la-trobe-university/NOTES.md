## Newly confirmed facts beyond existing card
- LIMS1 (the Lyons-designed original building) has "super labs": large, open, flexible shared
  research labs on its upper 3 of 6 floors, directly adjacent to write-up/office space -- distinctive
  hexagonal window motif repeated as an interior accent-wall design element.
- LIMS2 (where Bioimaging Platform specifically sits) is a separate but directly connected building
  (connected to LIMS1 on all levels, and to a TLC building on levels 2-4) -- same institute, so the
  interior design language (colour-coded accent walls, open-plan super-labs) very likely carries over,
  though this is an inference, not a confirmed fact about LIMS2 itself.
