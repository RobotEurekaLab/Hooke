Navy-blue accent wall, wood-veneer door, blue office chair, and a second partially-visible monitor at right. Clear room-level shot: door signage, walls, control desk, instrument, person.

Downloaded filenames: `light-microscopy.webp`, `electron-microscopy.webp`.

- No specific building/room number is legible in either photo (the TEM door sign is a generic equipment label, not a room number plaque), so this does not resolve the existing card's "no specific room-level address" gap, but it does visually confirm the facility is real, active, and multi-room.
