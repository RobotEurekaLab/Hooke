## What was found
**CRBA (Centro di Ricerca Biomedica Applicata / Centre for Applied Biomedical Research)** is a real, joint UniBo + Policlinico Sant'Orsola-Malpighi facility established 2001, with multiple core facilities (Animal Facility, Cellular Biology, Molecular Biology, Immunometry/Histochemistry, Histology/immunohistochemistry, SCAI-Lab, Mass Spectrometry, Biochemistry). The **Laboratorio di Biologia Cellulare (Cellular Biology Lab)** is the most concretely documented sub-unit:
- Address: Via Massarenti 9, Pad 20, 4th floor ("4° piano"), 40138 Bologna.
- Equipment: laminar flow hoods, hypoxic chambers, CO2 incubators, optical/fluorescence microscopes (incl. EVOS 5000), CytoFLEX flow cytometer, GloMax luminometer, Incucyte, ultracentrifuge.
No interior photos of this lab were found -- most of CRBA's own site is behind an SSO login wall.
