## What was found
No strong single "real lab" facility was confirmed for Sapienza by prior research. This pass identifies the **SNN-Lab (Sapienza Nanotechnology and Nanoscience Laboratory)**, run by CNIS, as the best candidate:
- Founded 2012, funded by Regione Lazio + Sapienza.
- ~400 sqm of recovered space in the "Ortopedia" building within Città Universitaria.
- 12 instruments, ~2 million EUR total value, across 5 functional/service areas (sample prep, light microscopy + image analysis, X-ray lab, materials testing lab, plus nano-fabrication/characterization).
No interior photographs were located for this facility within the search budget.
