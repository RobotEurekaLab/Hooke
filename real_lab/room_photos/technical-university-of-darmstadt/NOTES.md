## What was found -- CONFIRMED ROOM PHOTOS
Two genuine interior lab photos were found in the homepage's background
image carousel (`/media/aem/responsiv/bilder_24/buehnenbilder_6/`) and
downloaded:

1. **`arm200.jpg`** (from `ARM_200.jpg`, 1300x650) -- Wide shot of the JEOL
   ARM-200F transmission electron microscope column inside its room. Shows
   plain white/cream walls, a suspended ceiling with exposed cable trays and
   conduit, a doorway in the background, and a curved built-in control desk
   with three monitors (showing live TEM software), keyboard, a telephone,
   and the instrument's hand-control pendant. Radiation-hazard trefoil
   visible on the column housing. This is clearly the actual AEM microscope
   room, not a product shot.
2. **`leo_arm2.jpg`** (from `Leo__ARM_2.jpg`, 1300x650) -- A researcher
   (young man in a grey blazer, smiling, standing beside the same or a
   similar TEM column) with a cream/tan-walled room visible behind him,
   control desk and monitor (showing a live diffraction-pattern/image
   acquisition screen) to his right, instrument control pendant on the desk.

Both images show the room (walls, ceiling infrastructure, control desk,
multiple pieces of equipment/monitors together) rather than an isolated
product shot, and one includes a person -- satisfying the "room-level"
bar requested.

Two other candidate images from the same carousel were downloaded and
rejected as not useful: `Gruppe_9-20.jpg` (an outdoor group photo in a
forest -- a social/team event, not the lab) and
`Top_image_of_PZ_01_Kopie.jpg` (a black-and-white SEM micrograph of pollen
grains -- a scientific result image, not a room photo). Both were deleted.
