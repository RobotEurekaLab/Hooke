## What was found
Confirmed the SPS facility has two specific sintering machines (SPS-825, and SPS-530ET with integrated glovebox), established 2013 at the Department of Materials and Environmental Chemistry, staffed by Mirva Eriksson, and bookable via the LIMS system. No interior photo of the SPS room, or of any of Stockholm's other named chemistry core facilities (EMC, NMR, MACAL, CircuLab), was found. The Arrhenius Laboratory building itself is a notable piece of 1970s Swedish brutalist architecture (architect Carl Nyren, turning 50 in 2023 per a university social-media post), but available archival photos found in this pass were exterior/construction-era only.

## New facts beyond the existing card
