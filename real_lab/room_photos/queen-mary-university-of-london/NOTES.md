## What was found
The Genome Centre is confirmed to sit inside the Blizard Building, a RIBA-award-winning, purpose-built biomedical research building on QMUL's Whitechapel campus with an unusual architecture: a glass-box building with "pod" meeting rooms (designed to look like organic/cellular forms) suspended in a large central atrium/void, with open-plan research laboratories on the lower-ground floor lit by the void above.

Downloaded and visually confirmed **Blizard-interior.jpg**: a wide-angle interior shot looking down through the atrium showing one of the building's distinctive pod structures (a large, dark, whale/submarine-shaped pod suspended by angled black struts) with a lower-ground-floor open area visible below featuring rows of colorful (teal/orange) furniture or workstation dividers and what may be shelving or a communal area. This confirms the building's real, distinctive interior architecture, but it is a general atrium/circulation shot, not a close-up of the Genome Centre's own wet-lab benches.

## New facts beyond the existing card

## Follow-up: Flickr photo set (official Blizard Institute account)
Fetched https://www.flickr.com/photos/blizardinstitute/sets/72157687761650505 (official Blizard Institute Flickr album). It lists photo titles including "Blizard Institute Labs", "Inside the Blizard Laboratories", "A view of Spikey from the Blizard Laboratories", "Blizard Upper Floor", plus architectural/exterior shots (Cloud Pod, Mushroom, Footbridge, Perrin Lecture Theatre).

Downloaded the photo titled **"Inside the Blizard Laboratories"** (https://www.flickr.com/photos/blizardinstitute/36746028492/in/album-72157687761650505, credited to architectural photographer Morley von Sternberg) as **Inside-Blizard-Laboratories.jpg**. Viewed directly and confirmed it is an excellent, genuine room-level photo: looking through full-height glass doors into an open-plan wet lab on the **lower-ground floor, room sign reading "LG.121 Common"**. Visible: blue floor, blue-panelled fume hood/equipment unit on the left wall, white benches with cabinetry and open box/reagent shelving along both side walls, a trio of circular steel communal wash/eyewash basins set into a grey pedestal unit at the room's entrance, green-washed ceiling light panels, round porthole-style windows in a far interior wall, exposed ceiling services. This single photo gives genuine wall/floor/bench/shelving/room-sign detail for the exact type of open-plan lab space the Genome Centre (a core facility within this same building) occupies.
