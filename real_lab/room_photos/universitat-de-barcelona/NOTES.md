## What was found
The Jan 2025 inauguration event (President Salvador Illa touring CCiTUB's new electron microscope installation at Parc Científic de Barcelona) was documented in a 30-photo official UB Flickr album. Most photos are officials posing/walking; three stood out as genuine room-level shots:

- **jeol_cryo_tem_room.jpg**: The clearest confirmed room photo. Shows a clean, white-tiled microscopy suite -- white wall panelling, a tall column radiator, a wall-mounted AC split unit, overhead lighting, and a large JEOL-branded cryo-transmission electron microscope (visible logo "JEOL" on the column, plus a liquid-nitrogen/cryogen dewar unit to its left) with six people posed in front for a group photo. Real room: walls, ceiling, climate control, and the instrument together.
- **tem_room_detail.jpg**: A close-up/angled shot from inside the same room, showing a white-tiled ceiling with a square air vent, white wall panels, a floor-to-ceiling radiator, glass/aluminum partition framing, and 5 officials in suits standing inside -- confirms the room's finishes (fully tiled walls and ceiling, typical of a vibration/EM-sensitive clean installation).
- **presentation_auditorium.jpg**: A wide shot of a separate dark auditorium/event space at Parc Científic de Barcelona (not the lab itself) where a presentation slide is projected showing "Corrector d'aberracio esferica" (spherical aberration corrector) and "Detectors STEM" -- this is the event venue, not the lab room, but the projected slide itself contains a smaller inset photo of the actual microscope installation. Kept as supporting context, not counted as the primary room evidence.

Downloaded filenames: `jeol_cryo_tem_room.jpg`, `tem_room_detail.jpg`, `presentation_auditorium.jpg` (the last one plus the original hero image `inauguration_ccitub.jpg`, a corridor walk-through shot, kept for context but not room-level).

## Newly confirmed facts beyond existing card
- This is a newly identified concrete facility for Universitat de Barcelona (prior card had "none identified"): **CCiTUB (Centres Cientifics i Tecnologics de la Universitat de Barcelona)**, specifically its newly installed (inaugurated January 2025) first-generation JEOL cryo-electron microscope, housed at the **Parc Cientific de Barcelona (PCB)**.
- The room is a dedicated, recently fitted-out (tiled walls/ceiling, climate-controlled) EM suite -- distinct from CCiTUB's older NMR lab (also mentioned in search results as a separately recognized "Singular Scientific and Technological Infrastructure").
- Equipment brand confirmed: JEOL (visible logo on the microscope column in jeol_cryo_tem_room.jpg).

## Judgment calls
The Flickr album's photo IDs/ordering suggested roughly chronological sequence (entrance -> auditorium presentation -> lab tour -> group photos); only 6 of the 30 photos were sampled (time-boxed) rather than all 30 -- there are likely additional usable room photos in the same album if more time were invested later.
