## What was found
- The CISR main page's one image (`equipment-scaled-1-e1767979167829.jpg`)
  was downloaded and inspected: it shows a lab bench with a monitor
  displaying an obviously fake/mocked-up "Molecular search" software UI, a
  microscope, pipettes, and sample tubes, with a glass-walled room and
  shelving visible behind it. **This is almost certainly a generic stock/
  CGI photo** (the fake on-screen UI is a strong tell of stock photography
  used for web design), not an actual photo of the real CISR room --
  deleted after inspection rather than kept as a false positive.
- The VUMC news article about EM expansion contains only a headshot/portrait
  photo of facility director W. Gray Jerome, not a room photo.
- The Vanderbilt Magazine article contains only a colorized mass-spectrometry
  data visualization (a rat-brain lipid-distribution image), not a facility
  photo.
- CISR opened in 2016 as a Nikon Center of Excellence (one of only six in
  the US at the time) per a 2016 opening-ceremony news mention, but no photo
  from that event was located.

## Newly confirmed facts beyond existing card
- CISR operates as a Vanderbilt Nikon Center of Excellence, established
  2016, with support from the Dept. of Cell and Developmental Biology and
  the Office of the Dean of Basic Sciences, plus Nikon technical support.
