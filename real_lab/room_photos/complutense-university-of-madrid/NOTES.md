## Newly confirmed facts beyond existing card
- This is a newly identified concrete facility for Complutense (prior card had "none identified"): the **Flow Cytometry and Fluorescence Microscopy Unit**, part of **CAI (Centro de Asistencia a la Investigación) Biological Techniques**, located per its own page description "on the top floor of the National Center for Electron Microscopy, next to the Library of the Faculty of Chemistry at the Moncloa campus" (per WebSearch summary of https://cai.ucm.es/en/biological-techniques/flow-citometry-fluorescence-microscopy/).
- Specific named/modeled equipment confirmed via photo captions: FACSCelesta, FACSymphony A1, and FACSAria II (cell sorter) flow cytometers (Becton Dickinson/BD), a Cytek Aurora spectral flow cytometer, an Olympus FluoView-1200 laser confocal microscope, two Leica confocal microscopes (SP2-AOBS and SP-8), an Abberior superresolution STED microscope, a Zeiss AxioPlan-2 widefield fluorescence microscope, and an Eppendorf Centrifuge 5810 R visible in the room photo.
- A separate, larger facility (Madrid Advanced Microscopy Center, MAdMiC, a Euro-BioImaging node) also exists at UCM/Moncloa -- flagged as a lead for a possible future, even-stronger candidate, not pursued further here due to time budget.
