## What was found
Nanjing University's **现代分析中心 (Modern Analysis Center)**, now folded into the university's broader 科研设施共享中心 (Research Facilities Sharing Center), is a real, long-running (est. 1983) shared-instrumentation center:
- ~3000 sqm of lab space, >60 million RMB of imported precision instruments.
- 6 professional analytical labs: electron microscopy, NMR, energy spectrum (EDS), mass spectrometry, optical spectroscopy, physical properties.
- Address: Nanjing University Science Building (科学楼), 11-1 Beijing West Road (北京西路11-1号), Nanjing.
No interior photographs were found despite checking the center's own site, the university's news site, and the open-instrument-sharing platform.
