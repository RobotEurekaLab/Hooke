# University of St Andrews — room photo verification

Facility per existing card: School of Chemistry analytical facilities (NMR / Mass Spec / XRD), Purdie Building, North Haugh. Card specifically names the Liquid-state NMR service as being in **Room 122, ground floor, Purdie Building**.

- **https://www.connectnmruk.ac.uk/facility/university-of-st-andrews/** — UK National NMR network's facility page for St Andrews. Contains three genuine interior photographs of the spectrometers, confirmed room-level (not product shots) by direct visual inspection.

## What was found — CONFIRMED ROOM PHOTOS
Three photos downloaded and visually inspected:
- `nmr_600mhz.jpg` — Bruker Avance III HD 600 MHz system. Shows: light blue/pale blue painted walls, suspended ceiling tiles with recessed fluorescent lighting, a wood-veneer door, a glazed internal wall/corridor behind with a vending machine visible through it, blue flooring (carpet tile or vinyl), a gas cylinder with regulator, an electronics rack, a flat-panel monitor, EMI/safety signage, cardboard boxes on open shelving. Clearly a real multi-instrument room, not a single product shot.
- `nmr_500mhz.jpg` — Bruker Avance III HD + Ultrashield 500 system. Shows: cream/white walls, a ridged/corrugated false ceiling panel, twin fluorescent tube lighting, a wall clock, dark wood parquet flooring, a small wooden two-step stool used to reach the top of the magnet, loose cabling.
- `nmr_700mhz.jpg` — Bruker Ascend 700 system. Shows: cream walls, a window with horizontal venetian blinds (daylight visible through the slats), grey/blue speckled vinyl flooring, a cryogen-fill dewar on a wheeled trolley connected by a flexible transfer line, a PC/control unit.
All three rooms appear to be separate, adjoining rooms within the same NMR suite (consistent with "six spectrometers" described on the main facilities page), each a modest-sized room (roughly sized to fit one magnet + control electronics + a person), with plain painted/institutional decor, suspended or textured ceiling tiles, fluorescent lighting, and resilient (vinyl/carpet-tile) flooring rather than lab benches — consistent with dedicated magnet rooms rather than a general wet-lab.

## Newly confirmed facts beyond the existing card
- Visual confirmation that the NMR magnets are housed in individual small rooms (not open-plan), each sized for one magnet, matching the card's statement of "six spectrometers."
- Distinctive visible features: pale blue vs cream wall color differs by room; one room has a window with blinds (likely an exterior-facing room, probably the 700 MHz room); flooring differs between rooms (carpet tile vs parquet vs vinyl), suggesting the suite was fitted out over different renovation phases rather than as one uniform build.
- A floor plan/map image exists on the School's own NMR subsite (`Map.png`) confirming Room 122's location, though not retrieved as a file this round.

## Confidence tier
**Room photo confirmed.**
