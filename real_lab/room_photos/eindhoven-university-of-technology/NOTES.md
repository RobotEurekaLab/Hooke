# Eindhoven University of Technology -- TU/e Microfab Lab

Confidence tier: **room photo confirmed**

Source: https://www.microfab.tue.nl/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/csm_Microfab_2016_1109_BH-license-TUe_2b81969588.jpg
(saved as interior.jpg)

Shows: foreground bench with a real benchtop microscope (binocular), a
monitor showing fluorescence cell imaging, syringe pump arrays. Through a
glass partition behind it: an actual cleanroom bay with yellow safety
lighting, researchers in full bunny suits + hairnets, overhead articulated
equipment arms, stainless benches.

Newly confirmed facts (via https://research.tue.nl/en/equipments/microfablab/
and https://www.tue.nl/en/research/research-labs/microfablab):
- Exact location: Building 15, Gemini-Noord, Floor -1, de Zaale, Eindhoven.
- The cleanroom itself is named "Feynman".
- Facility is ~220 m2 total, across 6 research rooms including a bio lab.
