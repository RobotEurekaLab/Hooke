## URLs checked
- https://sites.google.com/keio.jp/eml/ — not re-fetched this pass (already confirmed text-only in existing card, full equipment list but no photos).
- https://photokichi.com/2024/05/18/65985245255/ — fetched directly. This is a professional architectural-photography blog ("Saito Photo Office," specializing in building/completion photography) with a dedicated post on exactly this building — the Keio University School of Medicine's Preventive Medicine building (予防医学教室), built 1929, RC 4-story, at 35 Shinanomachi, Shinjuku-ku. Two real building photos were found and downloaded; a third image URL referenced by the page's alt-text as "interior" was checked and found to actually be a second exterior/facade detail shot, not a true interior.
- Search for the Keio-Med Open Access (KOA) Facility (named as an alternative candidate in the existing card) — no page describing specific photos of its rooms was found; only general text about its mission as a cross-faculty shared-equipment coordination project (not a single physical lab space with a room to photograph).

## What was found — downloaded photo
- `keio_building_exterior_facade.jpg` (from https://photokichi.com/1/WP/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/00C8479-s.jpg): A close-up architectural-detail photograph of the building's carved stone entrance pediment, bearing the bilingual nameplate "慶應義塾大學醫學部 豫防醫學教室 / INSTITUTE OF PREVENTIVE MEDICINE, KEIO GIJUKU UNIVERSITY" with Art Deco-style ornamental carving (crossed-tool motif). This independently confirms the exact building's real, physical identity and its 1920s architectural style.
- A second image from the same post (full building exterior facade framed by trees/hedges, matching the search result's "RC造4階建" 4-story description) was also downloaded, reviewed, and discarded as redundant with the kept close-up nameplate shot once it became clear neither image shows the interior.

Both images are exterior-only. No interior shot of the actual 1st-floor Electron Microscope Center (or any interior of this building at all) was found in the blog post despite the page superficially describing one image as "interior" — on inspection this was also an exterior facade detail.

## Newly confirmed facts beyond existing card
- The Preventive Medicine Building is independently confirmed (via professional architectural photography, not just the facility's own page) to be a 1929, reinforced-concrete, 4-story Art Deco-style building, with a carved stone entrance nameplate reading "Institute of Preventive Medicine, Keio Gijuku University" — useful exterior/architectural-period detail not previously in the card, though it does not confirm anything about the EM Center's actual room interior.

## Confidence tier
**Location confirmed, no room photo.** A genuine, high-quality exterior photo of the correct building was found and kept as supplementary evidence (confirms building identity/period/style), but no interior photo of the Electron Microscope Center itself was located.
