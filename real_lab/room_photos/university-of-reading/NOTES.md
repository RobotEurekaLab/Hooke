## What was found
The Chemistry department's "facilities" page features two real photographs of the Chemical Analysis Facility, both downloaded and inspected:

- **caf_lab.jpg** ("Student using equipment in the Chemical Analysis Facility"): Clear room-level photo -- suspended ceiling tiles, venetian-blind-covered windows along the left wall, a structural pillar, and a large white NMR spectrometer magnet with an attached robotic/articulated sample-changer arm (autosampler) in the foreground; a student in a white lab coat and safety glasses is loading sample tubes into the autosampler's carousel. A second bench with further equipment is visible blurred in the background. Clearly a real, working room: walls, windows, pillar, multiple pieces of equipment, and a person at work.
- **using_lab_equipment.jpg** ("Person using lab equipment in the Chemical Analysis Facility"): A tighter, shallow-depth-of-field shot of a different person (lab coat, safety glasses) at an autosampler/vial-rack instrument, with a cream-colored wall and a wall-mounted vent panel visible softly in the background. Less of a wide establishing shot than caf_lab.jpg, but still shows wall/vent context plus the person and instrument.

Downloaded filenames: `caf_lab.jpg`, `using_lab_equipment.jpg`.
