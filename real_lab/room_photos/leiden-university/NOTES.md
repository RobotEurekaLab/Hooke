## What was found
NeCEN (Netherlands Centre for Electron Nanoscopy) is confirmed as a real, active national cryo-EM facility at **Gorlaeus Laboratory, Einsteinweg 55, 2333 CC Leiden**. It operates a BSL2 lab and separate microscope rooms, with (as of a 2026 search snippet) two Titan Krios transmission electron microscopes, one being newly replaced ("krios5" arriving Sept 2026). No interior photos of the microscope rooms or BSL2 lab were located within the search budget.

## Newly confirmed facts beyond the existing card
- As of September 2026, NeCEN is receiving a new Krios microscope ("krios5"), implying at least one of the two Krios bays is being actively upgraded/renovated around this time -- potentially relevant if reconstruction targets a "current" vs. "pre-upgrade" room state.
