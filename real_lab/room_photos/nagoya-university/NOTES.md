## What was found
The Live Imaging Center's own homepage (`liveimagingcenter/en/`) uses an image slider/carousel. Six slider images were downloaded and inspected directly (`images/slider-1.jpg` through `slider-6.jpg`). Two of them are genuine room-level interior photographs of the lab, not product shots or research-figure images:

1. **slider-2.jpg** (saved as `itbm_lab_bench_researcher.jpg`): A researcher in a white lab coat, ponytail, seated at a lab bench, looking into a stereo/dissecting microscope. Visible: white overhead cabinets with glass doors containing glassware/bottles, a monitor on an arm, an inverted microscope + camera rig to the right with a second monitor showing cell images, spray bottles, pipette tip boxes, a window with horizontal blinds in the background, black lab stool, dark countertop.

2. **slider-4.jpg** (saved as `itbm_microscope_room_zeiss.jpg`): A packed microscope room shot straight-on. Left bench holds a large white boxy instrument (plate reader or similar) plus an inverted microscope and a Zeiss-branded unit with "ZEISS" visible on the housing. Center: a flat monitor on a stand showing a loading/splash screen, with a second PC/keyboard station behind it. Right bench: multiple white upright/inverted microscopes with attached cameras and light sources (consistent with the confocal/multiphoton systems listed on the equipment page — LSM780, TCS SP8, CV1000, etc.), one branded "MBL 1000". Backdrop is a floor-to-ceiling gray pleated curtain (likely a blackout curtain for fluorescence work), dark non-reflective floor, gray/neutral walls. No windows visible in this shot — consistent with a dedicated darkroom-style imaging suite.

The other four slider images (1, 3, 5, 6) are all fluorescence microscopy *data* images (plant tissue, dividing cells, embryos) — i.e. research output, not room photos.
