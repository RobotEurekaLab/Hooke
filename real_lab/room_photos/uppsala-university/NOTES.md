## What was found
No interior/room photo of the BioVis facility (or of Rudbeck Laboratory's labs generally) was located this round. The handbook confirmed Rudbeck Laboratory is a multi-building complex (buildings labeled C11, R3, R4, plus MTC), with "larger labs" having their own deionised-water taps and a shared dry-ice store in building C11's nitrogen tank room (21110, K-floor) — i.e., genuine large wet-labs exist in the complex, but none specifically identified as the BioVis room.

## Newly confirmed facts beyond the existing card
- Rudbeck Laboratory's building-naming scheme uses letters+numbers (C11, R3, R4, MTC) distinct from the card's "BMC building B11" reference — these appear to be two different address/building-code systems (Rudbeck campus proper vs. the separate Biomedical Centre/BMC site mentioned on the BioVis page for some instruments). This is worth flagging as a possible point of confusion for future reconstruction work: BioVis's main site (Rudbeck) and its BMC B11 satellite instruments may be two distinct physical locations under different building-numbering conventions.
- Confirmed Rudbeck Laboratory is a large, multi-institution complex (500+ employees) rather than a single small building.
