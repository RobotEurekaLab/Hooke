01 · Real Lab Reconstruction

Not a generic bench — the actual room

For each university we went to the lab's own homepage, press materials, or facility photos and rebuilt the room against what's actually visible in them — wall color, shelving, windows, the specific instruments — instead of a plausible-looking guess. Every scene's sourcing is written down, including an honest tier for what's confirmed from a real photo versus representative where no photo exists.

02 · Space & Planetary Science

The same task, two physics engines, one answer

Sample loading, mass measurement and spectral analysis run identically on the Moon, Mars and in orbit — each task implemented once and executed on both MuJoCo and NVIDIA Isaac Sim (PhysX), so the two backends can be checked against each other rather than trusted on faith.

03 · Microscopy & Cell Manipulation

Micrometre scale, quantitative pass criteria

A 36 µm phantom cell, a 4 µm probe tip, forceps driven independently of each other — pushing, grasping and volume-controlled injection all score against explicit numeric thresholds (contact duration, placement error, injected volume) rather than a subjective "looks right."
