Scene Assets & Simulation Scope

Scenes combine generated craters or ridges, provenance-tracked LROC / HiRISE terrain tiles, and a NASA / SRB Apollo rock-sample mesh. The Martian rock sample uses the same mesh as a stand-in. The G1 model and gait policy come from a BSD-3-Clause open-source project, adapted here for low-gravity control. The G1 handles walking and contact confirmation while the rover handles grasping; terrain is a rigid surface and navigation uses state feedback. Loose regolith, dust, vision-based autonomous navigation, and humanoid rock grasping are not yet implemented.

Footage is from live simulation. Full-mission videos are only published for runs that pass the collection-and-return check; new deployments can open the scene or start a run directly.
