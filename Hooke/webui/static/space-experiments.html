Experiment scope
Mass measurement uses actual spring-slide motion and encoder curves, including empty-load, reference, and unknown samples. The space station uses inertial measurement; the Moon and Mars use elastic-support measurement.
The spectral task uses explicitly generated analytic test curves, supporting dark-field, reference, repeated measurements, and rejection of unknown curves; the current data is not a USGS material spectrum, nor real lunar or Martian geological composition.
Samples are closed rigid-body geometry; airtightness, vacuum material compatibility, airflow, dust, and station-body recoil are not yet simulated. Fixture locking is an explicit constraint; robot grasping has no auxiliary holding constraint.
