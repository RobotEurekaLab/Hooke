Validation

Loading results…

Source triangle counts, material grouping, UV, normals, and diffuse textures have been checked. Full PBR and lighting consistency have not yet been validated.

Collisions use convex hulls for rock samples, capsules for sample tubes and handrails, and boundary panels for the module. Sample mass of 0.1 kg is an example setting. Lunar and Martian terrain are still procedural prototypes. Airflow, leaks, phase changes, dust, and mechanical lid-opening have not yet been implemented.
